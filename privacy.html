Privacy Policy

Bonk Road (iOS, Android and browser) · Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Türkçe

In short

Bonk Road shows no ads, collects no advertising ID and does not sell your data. It keeps only the minimum needed to run the game and save your progress.

Data we collect

  • Player ID: a random guest ID is created for you the first time you open the game.
  • Game progress: stage, upgrades, gear, in-game currencies, quest and reward records.
  • Activity day: the days on which you opened the game (date only; used to measure balance and player return).
  • If you link an account: your email address; with Google or Apple, the account identifier and email address that provider gives us. We never see your password; with email sign-in your password is stored only as a hash.

We do not access location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers. We use no third-party analytics or advertising software.

How we use it

  • To save your progress and carry it across devices,
  • to prevent cheating (progress is verified on the server),
  • to produce aggregate, non-personal numbers that help us balance the game.

Where it is stored and who receives it

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider Supabase on servers in the European Union (Ireland). If you sign in with Google or Apple, authentication goes through those companies. We share your data with no other third party, except where the law requires it.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled locally on your device; no notification identifier is sent to our server.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. In the game, Account → Delete my account and data removes your account and all records immediately and permanently. Details: Delete account and data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights and contact

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. For questions and requests, use the developer contact address on the game's App Store / Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
