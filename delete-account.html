Delete account and data

Bonk Road (iOS, Android and browser) · Türkçe

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Bonk Road.
  2. Go to the Account tab at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data at the bottom and confirm twice.

Your account and all data tied to it are deleted from the server immediately and permanently: player ID, linked email and Google/Apple identity, game progress, in-game currencies, quest and reward records, activity days. Deleted data cannot be restored. No other data is retained.

If you cannot access the app

Send a message with the subject "Bonk Road account deletion" from the email address linked to your account to the developer contact address on the game's App Store / Google Play page. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days at the latest.

Resetting only the device

If you never linked an account, your progress belongs to a guest session on your device; deleting the app removes that session from the device. Use the button above to delete the server record as well.
